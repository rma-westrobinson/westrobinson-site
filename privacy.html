Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026   |   Last updated: July 2026

West Robinson Group ("we," "us," "our") operates westrobinson.com. This policy describes what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it. It applies to all visitors, regardless of location, and is written to satisfy Canada's federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

What information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us. When you contact us through the site, whether by email, through a booking form, or by scheduling a call, we receive the information you provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your organization or institution
  • The content of your message or inquiry
  • A preferred time or date for a call, if provided

We do not collect payment information, health records, or any sensitive personal information through this site.

What we do not collect automatically

This site does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics scripts that identify individual visitors. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate, anonymized traffic data (page views, referral sources, general geography). Cloudflare does not use cookies and does not build individual profiles. No personal information is collected in this process.

How we use your information

Information you send us is used exclusively to respond to your inquiry and, if an engagement follows, to carry out that work. We do not use your contact details for marketing, do not add you to mailing lists without explicit consent, and do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

Third-party services

This site uses Calendly to facilitate appointment booking. If you book a call through the scheduling widget, your name, email address, and selected time are processed by Calendly in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at calendly.com/legal/privacy-notice. You are not required to use Calendly; you may also contact us directly at contact@westrobinson.com.

Email correspondence is processed through our email provider. We recommend you do not include sensitive personal or health information in an initial inquiry.

How long we keep your information

We retain inquiry records for as long as they are reasonably necessary to respond to your request or conduct an engagement. Where no engagement follows an initial inquiry, we generally delete contact records within twelve months. You may request earlier deletion at any time (see Your rights below).

Where your information is held

West Robinson Group operates in Ontario, Canada. Email and calendar data may be processed by service providers whose servers are located outside Canada, including in the United States. We use providers whose data handling practices are consistent with applicable Canadian privacy law.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request a copy of that information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent to our holding your information (subject to legal or contractual obligations)
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, for Quebec residents, the Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.quebec.ca)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly. We will respond within thirty days.

Children

This site is not directed at individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact

Questions, access requests, or deletion requests can be directed to the Principal of West Robinson Group, who serves as the responsible party for personal information under this policy.
