Practice

Practice areas

Engagements cover any point in the medication management continuum where safety, compliance, or operational performance is at risk, whether that's a survey date, an inspection finding, a cabinet configuration, a bedside scanning workflow, or an EHR pharmacy build. Roger scopes and leads every engagement personally, and brings in vetted specialist consultants when a platform or system calls for expertise beyond his own.

Accreditation readiness
Medication management standards
Survey preparation built around Accreditation Canada's medication management standards, including high-alert medication controls and ISMP Canada reconciliation requirements. Prepared from four vantage points, as hospital director, senior leadership, board member, and external consultant.
Medication management policy
Regulatory & operational
Full medication management policy suite development and maintenance, covering controlled substance handling, high-alert medications, medication error reporting, storage standards, prescriber order documentation, and reconciliation processes. Built to satisfy both Accreditation Canada standards and OCP inspection requirements.
Drug shortage & procurement support
Supply chain
Sourcing and procurement support for backordered, discontinued, and hard-to-find medications, drawing on established supplier relationships and cross-institutional networks. Particularly valuable for surgical and specialty settings where formulary gaps carry direct patient safety implications.
Infusion pump drug library management
Clinical technology
Drug library build, review, and maintenance across multiple infusion pump platforms including Plum 360, Baxter Sigma Spectrum, and Medfusion 4000. Covers concentration standardization, dosing limit validation, and soft and hard stop configuration aligned to ISMP Canada guidelines.
P&T committee support
Formulary governance
Ongoing pharmacy and therapeutics committee advisory support, including therapeutic interchange proposals, formulary addition and deletion reviews, renal dose adjustment authority frameworks, and nurse-initiated protocol development. Structured for both recurring retainer and project-based engagements.
Formulary management
Medication management
Formulary structure, review processes, and governance frameworks for hospitals and health systems managing drug approval and utilization decisions.
Antimicrobial stewardship
Clinical program design
Stewardship program design and operational rebuild, including monthly days-of-therapy trending and real-time physician rounding, built to shift programs from retrospective review to active intervention. Demonstrated outcome: vancomycin use reduced from 71.7 to 27.9 DOT per 1,000 patient days.
Licensure and policy framework
Regulatory readiness
Medication management policy development for organizations seeking or renewing licensure, including controlled substance chain-of-custody, prescriber order documentation, medication error classification, and storage protocols. Relevant for addiction medicine, recovery, and specialty clinic settings outside traditional hospital pharmacy.
EHR & connected health record advisory
Health informatics
Independent clinical review of connected health record and EHR pharmacy modules ahead of go-live, scoped to medication reconciliation, patient-specific medication selection, and pharmacy verification workflows. For platform-specific build work, Roger coordinates vetted specialist consultants from his network under a single point of accountability.
Discharge documentation systems
Clinical informatics
Order sets and discharge prescription documentation designed for clarity at the point of use, including structured checkbox-driven formats for surgical and acute care settings.
Governance advisory
Board & senior leadership
Advisory support for boards and senior leadership teams on pharmacy-related governance questions, drawing on direct board experience across nephrology, geriatric care, and Ontario Health Teams.

Roger Ma leads West Robinson Group's engagements directly. He is Director of Pharmacy at two Ontario hospitals, where he manages medication management across the full institution, from formulary governance and controlled substance policy to bedside scanning, automated dispensing cabinets, and clinical informatics, for systems that answer to the same accreditation and College standards his clients face. His consulting work extends into connected health record and EHR pharmacy module reviews for hospital systems preparing for go-live, and into policy framework development for specialty and recovery clinic settings pursuing licensure.

The network behind West Robinson Group brings more than 50 years of collective medication management experience across acute care, community health, and health informatics. Prior to founding the practice, Roger co-founded Nephron Health, a healthtech company that won $200,000 at the DMZ Ryerson COVID-19 business technology competition, and served as Director of Client Care, Decision Support, and Information Technology at one of the largest ethnospecific seniors and community services organizations in Ontario, with accountability for a $13M operating budget across dialysis, transitional care, homecare, and chronic disease management.

His board experience spans nephrology, geriatric care, long-term care, mental health outreach, Ontario Health Teams, and the United Nations Association of Canada, Toronto. That range puts him on both sides of the table, as the operator who has to make a policy work on the floor, and as the board member who has to be satisfied it will hold up under scrutiny.

He holds a PharmD from the University of Toronto and is completing an MBA at the Gies College of Business at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, layered on nineteen completed certificate programs in data science, financial strategy, and healthcare business. That training is what sits behind the EHR and connected health record advisory work, where reviewing a pharmacy module build takes data fluency alongside clinical judgment.
